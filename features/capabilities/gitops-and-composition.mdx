> Your GitOps repository is the only source of desired state. The GitOps engine makes the box match it, and composition turns each declaration into the resources behind it.

## What it gives you

- **Reconciliation.** The GitOps engine on the management cluster reconciles everything in the box from your GitOps repository. An agent on each managed cluster applies what it is given.
- **Cluster registration.** Each cluster the platform creates is registered with the GitOps engine as it appears.
- **Composition.** The platform's own declarations, such as an application or a database, become the set of resources that realise them.
- **Configuration reloads.** A workload restarts when a secret or config map it reads changes.

## What you own

The contents of your GitOps repository. Every change to what runs reaches your clusters as a commit there.
