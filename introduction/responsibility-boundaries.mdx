> Kamarkat provides the platform. You own the box it runs in, and everything you build on it.

Kamarkat's part is the bundle: every platform component, its tested versions and defaults, the templates you start from, and the `kakt` CLI. Everything else is yours: your accounts, your GitOps repository, your clusters, your applications, and the use you make of every capability.

Kamarkat is built for teams that have no platform team, so there is no split between a platform team and engineering teams: everything outside the bundle is yours to decide.

You do hold accounts with other providers — your cloud, your git host, your container registry, the secret manager your escrow lives in, and an alert endpoint if you configure one. Each is yours, contracted by you and revocable by you. Kamarkat holds credentials to none of them.

Each row links to the page that describes it in detail.

## Git layer

| | Kamarkat | You |
| :- | :- | :- |
| **[Platform content](/features/box/bundle)** | Publishes each bundle version with its defaults | Choose the version each cluster runs |
| **[GitOps repository](/features/admin/git-connection)** | Provides the template it is created from | Own it: your values, cluster declarations and app declarations |
| **[Upgrades](/versions/overview)** | Publishes new versions | Take them by changing the version your cluster pins |
| **[Application repositories](/features/workloads/deploying-applications)** | Publishes the `platform-workload` library chart | Own your source, charts, images and CI |

## Agentic operations layer

| | Kamarkat | You |
| :- | :- | :- |
| **Platform agents** | Ships the agents and the platform model they operate against | Decide which run in your box, and remain accountable for what they do |
| **MCP registry** | Publishes the tools and integrations agents may discover | Authorize what is available in your box |
| **Skills registry** | Ships reusable skills carrying the platform's conventions | Add your own, and decide which apply |
| **What an agent may reach** | Exposes capability through authorized, standardized platform contracts | Decide what is authorized in your box, and own the credentials and policy behind it |

Agentic operations do not add a second authority. An agent works through the same platform contracts and within the same permissions as anyone else operating the box, so nothing in the table above moves a boundary that exists elsewhere on this page.

| :- | :- | :- |
| **[Cloud account](/features/admin/cloud-account)** | — | Own it, pay for it, hold its credentials |

## What you need to know

### The line is authorship, not location

A component belongs to Kamarkat when none of your logic is inside it. A database engine is Kamarkat's; the tables in it are yours. Where it runs does not change that.
