This quickstart takes you from empty accounts to an application served on your own domain. You run every step yourself, under your own credentials. zero-ops never holds them.

## What you will build

- A **tenant repository**, `<tenant>-gitops`, in your git organisation. It is the only source of desired state for your box.
- A **management cluster** that runs your control plane.
- A **workload cluster** that runs your applications.
- One **application**, deployed from its own repository and served on a hostname under your domain.

## How it fits together

<Steps>
  <Step title="Prerequisites">
    Prepare a cloud account, a git organisation, a domain and an escrow, and install the `soloz` CLI.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build your first cluster">
    Create the escrow project, scaffold your tenant repository, and run Day-0 from that repository's own CI. Day-0 creates the management cluster, which then provisions the workload cluster.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run your first app">
    Declare the application in your tenant repository, supply its secrets, and let its build publish and pin its chart.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How long it takes

Most of the time goes into Day-0, which creates real infrastructure and runs for a few hours. The steps before and after it take minutes.

<Note>
Creating clusters costs money in your cloud account. Day-0 runs only when you start it, never because a file changed.
</Note>
