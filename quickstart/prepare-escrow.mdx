Your box's secret store is encrypted by master keys. The box cannot keep them itself: they are what decrypts it, so they would be unreachable exactly when you need them. The escrow keeps a copy outside the box, in a secret manager you control and Kamarkat does not.

The escrow also holds each cluster's admin kubeconfig, which you use only for [break-glass access](/quickstart/access-your-box#break-glass-access), and the cloud credential your box's infrastructure is built and destroyed with.

You reach it as **yourself**. Step 2 below signs you in, and from then on every command that touches the escrow uses your own session rather than a credential copied onto your machine.

<Warning>
Without an escrow, losing the management cluster loses every secret the platform manages for it. You cannot add an escrow afterwards, so scaffolding refuses to continue without one.
</Warning>

## Step 1: Create an account and a machine identity

Create an account on the escrow secret manager (by default `https://app.infisical.com`), then create one **machine identity**. Note its client ID and client secret.

## Step 2: Sign in as yourself

```bash
kakt login
```

A browser login against the escrow's Infisical — the account you just created. The session is kept in your operating system's credential store, not in a file, and no box ever holds a copy.

```bash
kakt whoami
```

```text
you@acme.example at https://app.infisical.com
  identity      3f9c1b7e-...-a21d
  organization  org_abc123
  session kept in macOS keychain
```

**Keep that `identity` value.** It is Infisical's own id for your account, and the next page declares it as the box's platform administrator — the one human the box accepts for escrow-backed operations. An address is not used, because an address can be changed and re-assigned.

<Note>
The machine identity from Step 1 is for **automation** — your box's operator and your CI use it to escrow recovery material as they run. It is never a substitute for signing in, and `kakt` prefers your session whenever you have one. [Platform administrator and access](/documentation/escrow/platform-administrator) covers both.
</Note>

## Step 3: Create the escrow project

```bash
kakt escrow init \
  --client-id <machine-identity-client-id> \
  --client-secret <machine-identity-client-secret>
```

This creates the project your boxes escrow to and adds the machine identity to it as an admin. Use `--url` if your escrow is not at the default address.

## Step 4: Export the escrow values

```bash
export INFISICAL_ESCROW_URL=https://app.infisical.com
export INFISICAL_ESCROW_PROJECT_ID=<project-id>
export INFISICAL_ESCROW_CLIENT_ID=<machine-identity-client-id>
export INFISICAL_ESCROW_CLIENT_SECRET=<machine-identity-client-secret>
```

Keep these values somewhere safe outside the box. They are what your box's automation authenticates with; your own access comes from `kakt login`, so you do not need them to operate the box day to day.
