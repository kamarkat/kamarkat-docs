> Kamarkat provides the platform and the tooling that installs it. You own the box it runs in, and everything you build on it.

## What Kamarkat handles, and what stays under your control

### Installation

`kakt` collects the credentials your box is built with, creates your escrow project and your GitOps repository, and declares your management cluster. It keeps the credentials in a local credential store on your machine and sets the ones CI needs as encrypted CI secrets on your repository; the repository itself holds declarations, not plaintext credentials. Day-0 then builds that cluster and brings up the platform on it. You authorize each step: you supply every credential, you commit the declaration, and you start the Day-0 run from your own CI. Nothing is built until you start it.

### Continuing operation

Your management cluster runs the platform's services and reconciles the box from your GitOps repository. It provisions the managed clusters you declare and deploys the applications you declare. You decide what is declared, and you remain responsible for keeping the box healthy: its capacity, its cost and the incidents it raises.

### Platform upgrades

Kamarkat publishes each platform version as one tested bundle. The upgrade automation configured in your repository proposes it as a pull request, and a pre-flight check in your box judges it against the cluster it targets. Nothing changes until you merge.

Reverting a platform-version declaration changes the desired state that GitOps reconciles. It does not guarantee reversal of database migrations, deleted resources or other stateful changes. Each release page says whether going back restores the previous state; use the supported [rollback](/guides/platform/roll-back-a-version) or recovery procedure for the affected version.

### Your applications

The platform renders each application's namespace, gateway route, login, database, secrets and network policy from your declaration. Your code, images, configuration, data and secret values are yours, and so is how you use each capability.

### Recovery

Kamarkat separates recovery material from the infrastructure it protects. Your box's master keys and each cluster's break-glass credential are kept in an escrow you own, outside the box, and `kakt` ships the commands that verify the escrow and recover with what it holds.

The escrow alone does not rebuild a box. Reconstructing a management cluster also needs your GitOps declarations and the management-state archives in your object storage, and it follows the [replacement procedure](/guides/box/replace-a-management-cluster). Application data follows its own recovery procedures, from the database backups you configure. You keep the escrow, the repository and the backups, and you decide when to use them.

## In detail

Kamarkat's part is the bundle: every platform component, its tested versions and defaults, the templates you start from, and the `kakt` CLI. Everything else is yours: your accounts, your GitOps repository, your clusters, your applications, and the use you make of every capability.

Kamarkat is built for teams that have no platform team, so there is no split between a platform team and engineering teams: everything outside the bundle is yours to decide.

You do hold accounts with other providers: your cloud, your git host, your container registry, the secret manager your escrow lives in, and an alert endpoint if you configure one. Each is yours, contracted by you and revocable by you. Every credential to them is one you supply, and Kamarkat's own services receive none of them.

## Agentic operations layer

<Note>
The agentic layer is in development. This table records how responsibility is designed to divide once it ships.
</Note>

| | Kamarkat | You |
| :- | :- | :- |
| **Platform agents** | Ships the agents and the platform model they operate against | Decide which run in your box, and remain accountable for what they do |
| **MCP registry** | Publishes the tools and integrations agents may discover | Authorize what is available in your box |
| **Skills registry** | Ships reusable skills carrying the platform's conventions | Add your own, and decide which apply |
| **What an agent may reach** | Exposes capability through authorized, standardized platform contracts | Decide what is authorized in your box, and own the credentials and policy behind it |

Agentic operations do not add a second authority. An agent works through the same platform contracts and within the same permissions as anyone else operating the box, so nothing in the table above moves a boundary that exists elsewhere on this page.

## What you need to know

### The line is authorship, not location

A component belongs to Kamarkat when none of your logic is inside it. A database engine is Kamarkat's; the tables in it are yours. Where it runs does not change that.
