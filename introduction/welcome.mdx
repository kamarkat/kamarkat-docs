**Kamarkat — Agentic Cloud-Native Platform OS for Businesses**

## Your entire cloud-native platform. One OS. Built for humans and AI agents.

Kamarkat is an open-source, agentic cloud-native platform OS that brings infrastructure, application services and intelligent operations together in one integrated platform.

From Kubernetes and GitOps to identity, secrets, databases, networking and observability, the infrastructure your applications depend on works as a coordinated system.

With integrated AI agents, an MCP registry and reusable operational skills, Kamarkat gives businesses a consistent way to understand, manage and evolve their platform — all on infrastructure they own.

## The platform at a glance

Three layers of one product, not three things to buy:

```text
┌─ Agentic operations ──────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  Agents · MCP registry · Skills registry                             │
│  How engineers and AI agents inspect, investigate and operate         │
└───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
┌─ Integrated cloud-native platform OS ─────────────────────────────────┐
│  GitOps · Identity · Secrets · Databases · Gateway · Networking       │
│  Observability · Application delivery · Cluster lifecycle             │
│  Versioned together, reconciled from your repository                  │
└───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
┌─ Business-owned infrastructure ───────────────────────────────────────┐
│  Management cluster · Workload clusters · Cloud infrastructure        │
│  Storage · Networking                                                 │
└───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

The agentic layer does not sit beside the platform with its own view of the world. It reads the same platform state engineers do, and when desired state changes, it changes through the same declarative contracts the platform reconciles.

## What arrives integrated

| | |
|---|---|
| **Cluster lifecycle** | Declare a cluster and the platform provisions it, brings up its networking, storage and delivery, registers it, scales it and removes it |
| **GitOps delivery** | Your repository is the desired state; platform controllers reconcile to it |
| **Databases** | PostgreSQL per application, with credentials, backups and recovery procedures. *Selectable, on by default* |
| **Identity** | An identity provider in your box, and a gateway that fronts your applications with login |
| **Secrets** | A secret store, delivery into workloads, and recovery material held outside the box |
| **Networking, DNS, certificates** | Gateway routing, network policy, DNS records, certificates issued and renewed |
| **Observability** | Metrics, logs, dashboards and alert routing across the box. *Selectable, on by default* |
| **Agentic operations** | Platform agents, a registry of the tools they may use, and reusable skills that carry the platform's own conventions |

Packaging these would be a distribution. **Pre-integrating them is the product:** the gateway knows about your identity provider, an application's chart gets its database and its secrets, a new cluster arrives with its networking and delivery already configured, and one version moves them together.
