Every business building cloud-native products ends up building a platform. Usually without deciding to, and now with a third problem the first two never had.

## Three connected challenges

### Infrastructure assembly

A complete cloud-native platform is a dozen technologies that have to agree with each other: a cluster and its networking, delivery, a secret store, certificates, DNS, a database, an identity provider, a gateway, dashboards. Each has its own configuration surface, its own dependencies and its own release cadence.

The integration is the work. Installing the components is the easy half, and the half that looks finished.

### Continuous operational complexity

The platform does not stop needing attention once it exists. Deployments, incidents, security updates, platform changes and infrastructure maintenance all compete with building the product — and the competition is permanent, not a phase.

### Fragmented agentic operations

This one is new, and it is where most platforms have nothing to offer.

AI agents are becoming part of how teams operate infrastructure. An agent pointed at an independently assembled platform meets inconsistent tools, conventions, permissions and workflows — each component with its own interface, its own idea of identity, its own notion of what an operation is.

So every business ends up doing the same undifferentiated work twice: once to assemble its platform, and again to establish how agents understand and operate it.

## Kamarkat's answer

A standardized cloud-native operating foundation where the infrastructure and platform services are already integrated, and where agents interact with platform capabilities through the same operational contracts engineers use.

| You provide | Kamarkat provides |
|---|---|
| Your cloud account and your domain | The provisioning, lifecycle and reconciliation that run on them |
| Your requirements and configuration | An integrated platform whose components are versioned and tested as one set |
| Your application logic | The routing, identity, database, secrets and observability it connects to |
| Your operational judgement | A platform agents can discover and operate through declared, authorized capabilities |

The division is deliberate: the business keeps what distinguishes it, and stops rebuilding the foundation every business needs.

## What this does not claim

Infrastructure still needs operating, and agents do not change who is accountable for it. What changes is how much of the platform you assemble, how consistent it is to operate, and whether an agent working on it is improvising against a stack nobody standardized.
