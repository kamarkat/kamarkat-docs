> The Credit Authority is a ledger for a box that grants and spends credits.

## What it gives you

- **A credit ledger** for the box, with transactional guarantees.
- **A route for it,** `credits.<hubDomain>`, through the gateway.

## How to turn it on

Credits are selectable and off by default:

```yaml
capabilities:
  credits:
    enabled: true
```

An application consumes it by declaring `capabilities.credits` in its own values.

## Built on

| Project | Role in your box | Runs on |
| :- | :- | :- |
| **Formance Ledger** | Records every credit grant and spend | Management cluster |
| **agentgateway** | Serves the ledger's route | Management cluster |
