> Kamarkat may propose changes to your platform. It is never the authority that makes them take effect.

## What it is

A box is everything Kamarkat gives you: a **management cluster** that runs the platform's services, and the **managed clusters** it creates and reconciles. It runs in your cloud account, under credentials you hold, and it belongs to exactly one tenant.

The platform is **delivered, not hosted**. It is the same platform Kamarkat tests and publishes, not a reduced edition. Once it is running it reconciles your clusters from your own repository and depends on nothing Kamarkat operates.

## What Kamarkat never holds

| Credential | Where it lives |
| :- | :- |
| Cloud provider credentials | Your management cluster |
| Cluster admin credentials | Your box, with a break-glass copy escrowed in your own cloud account |
| Secret material | Your box's secret store |
| Write access to your repository | Your own automation |

Because no Kamarkat component serves more than one tenant, a compromise of Kamarkat cannot reach your running infrastructure. Isolation between customers comes from the topology itself: there is no shared management cluster to break into.

## How a change reaches your box

1. Kamarkat **publishes** a new platform version.
2. You **take** it by changing the version a cluster pins, in that cluster's `bundle.yaml`.
3. A **pre-flight check** judges that version against the cluster it targets, and reports a verdict rather than applying anything.
4. Your management cluster **reconciles** the new version from your repository.

Kamarkat does step 1 and nothing else. Every change reaches running infrastructure through your repository and your management cluster.

## Reaching your box

You sign in to your box as a person, through your box's identity provider. The cluster admin credential is a **break-glass artefact**: it is escrowed outside the box, in your own cloud account, and used only when ordinary access has failed.

## What you need to know

### Your box keeps running without Kamarkat

If Kamarkat is unreachable, your box keeps reconciling. Nothing is ever withdrawn from it, because nothing was ever held on your behalf.

### Kamarkat cannot see inside your box

No Kamarkat component opens a connection into your box, holds a credential for it, or queries it.

### Every box carries its own management cluster

There is no shared management cluster between tenants. That is what makes the custody claim true, and it sets the floor on what a box costs.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can Kamarkat push a change to my clusters?">
    No. Kamarkat holds no access to your repository or your clusters. A change takes effect only when your repository says so and your management cluster applies it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What if a published version is defective?">
    A published version is never changed in place. Kamarkat publishes a new version that supersedes it, and that version reaches you as a proposal like any other.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
