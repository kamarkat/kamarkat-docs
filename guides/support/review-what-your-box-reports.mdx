> Read the payload before you enrol. Nothing leaves until you do.

The Support Agent runs on every cluster and sends nothing until you enrol. These commands show what it may send and what it would send. Neither transmits anything.

## Step 1: See what may leave

```bash
kakt support scope
```

Every field that may leave the box: the platform's allowlist, minus anything you denied. Nothing outside this list can be sent. The list widens only when a new platform version adds to it, which reaches you as an upgrade you review.

## Step 2: See what it would send today

```bash
kakt support preview
```

Runs every collector in scope against this cluster and prints the result; `--json` prints it as JSON. A collector that could not run is listed with its reason, never left out.

## Step 3: Subtract what you will not send

```bash
kakt support scope --deny <collector>
kakt support preview --deny <collector>.<field>
```

`--deny` removes a whole collector, or one field of it, and the output shows the scope with that applied.
