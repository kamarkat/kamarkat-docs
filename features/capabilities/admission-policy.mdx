> Every cluster checks what it is asked to run before it runs it.

## What it gives you

- **Admission checks** on everything a cluster admits, including required labels and images pinned by digest.
- **The same rules on every cluster,** delivered with each bundle version.

## What you own

Meeting the rules in your charts. The platform workload library renders compliant objects; see [Deploying applications](/features/workloads/deploying-applications).
