> Every hostname gets a public TLS certificate, issued and renewed for you, and the box runs its own internal certificate authority.

## What it gives you

- **Public TLS certificates** for every gateway and webhook, issued and renewed automatically.
- **Domain ownership proof** through your DNS provider.
- **An internal certificate authority** for certificates used inside the box.

## What you own

Your domain and its DNS zone; see [Cloud account](/features/admin/cloud-account).

## Built on

| Project | Role in your box | Runs on |
| :- | :- | :- |
| **cert-manager** | Issues and renews certificates | Both cluster types |
| **cert-manager DNS-01 webhook** | Proves domain ownership through the provider's DNS | Both cluster types |
| **Infisical PKI issuer** | Issues internal certificates from the secret store's certificate authority | Both cluster types |

Versions are not listed here: each release lists the exact version of every component it installs, under [Versions](/versions/overview) › **Releases**.
